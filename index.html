06

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Talk to us.

Do my clients need to download an app?

No. Clients book from a simple web page that opens on any phone. Only you and your staff use the Slotra app.

Can I still take walk-ins and phone bookings?

Yes. Add walk-ins and phone bookings to the same calendar in two taps, so online and offline bookings never clash.

How do WhatsApp reminders work?

Slotra sends a confirmation as soon as a booking is made, then reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before. You can change the timing and the message.

Is my clients' and patients' data safe?

All data is encrypted and backed up daily. Only you and the staff you allow can see client records and notes.

Can I switch plans later?

Any time. Upgrades apply immediately, and downgrades apply from your next billing date.
